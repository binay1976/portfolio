About Me

A little about myself

⭐ “For me, the real reward is turning ideas into action.”

My professional journey is a blend of mechanical engineering, product innovation, railway operations, and digital transformation.

After joining Western Railway, I gained firsthand experience in locomotive operations. Working in this environment helped me recognize an opportunity: the potential to use digital tools to make learning, operational processes, and access to information more effective for Loco Pilots and railway personnel.

What started as an effort to solve small, practical problems gradually evolved into developing digital tools, dashboards, automation solutions, and web applications designed to simplify railway workflows and save time.

Today, I bring together my engineering mindset, operational experience, and self-driven IT learning to develop solutions grounded in real-world requirements. My approach remains simple: understand the problem, build a practical solution, and make technology genuinely useful to the people who need it.

THE STORY BEHIND THE DESIGNATIONS

Engineering Mindset. Operational Experience. Digital Innovation.

My professional journey began in Mechanical Engineering, where I learned to transform ideas into practical products. As an R&D Engineer at SLEEK International, Mumbai, I worked on modular kitchen accessories, product designs, fittings, and installation. I also contributed to production automation, correcting design flaws and improving manufacturing processes to save time, money, and manpower.

In 2003, I joined Western Railway as an Assistant Loco Pilot. Working in railway operations gave me firsthand experience of the challenges faced by running staff. I realized that complex railway yards, signals, points, operational information, and recurring reporting requirements could benefit from practical digital tools.

At a time when learning IT independently was difficult, I started from the basics, using books and online resources while continuing my regular railway duties. I began developing small tools to address real operational problems. What started as individual solutions gradually evolved into interactive simulators, quizzes, data collection systems, dashboards, and analytical applications.

Today, as a Chief Loco Inspector, I continue to combine railway domain knowledge with data analysis and application development. My objective remains the same: understand the problem at its source, build a practical solution, and make technology useful to the people who need it.

2010 – 2011 EARLY DIGITAL INNOVATION

My First Railway Digital Learning Tools

Making complex railway layouts easier to understand.

Loco Pilots traditionally needed to walk through railway yards to learn routes, signals, points, and track arrangements. Remembering an entire complex layout was challenging, and field visits did not always allow staff to draw diagrams or make detailed notes conveniently.

I developed the Yard Simulator to let users explore and simulate railway yard layouts, understand the routes associated with signals, and visualize train movements. I subsequently developed the LRD Quiz as a way to test crew knowledge.

2017 WORKFLOW DIGITALIZATION

e-TRO — A Unified Platform for Data Collection

Bringing distributed reporting workflows together.

Data collection and report preparation involved information from different field units, including CLIs, CTCC, TLC, running rooms, and loco sheds. Collecting and compiling this information for routine reporting could create avoidable coordination and administrative effort.

I developed e-TRO as a common platform where field personnel could submit information through forms and access reports. The system helped streamline data collection and the preparation of PCDO, MCDO, and other day-to-day reports required by officers and departments.

Operational contribution
