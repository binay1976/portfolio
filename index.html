01 Crew Training

Yard Simulator

Visualising Railway Yards for Better Route Familiarity

A digital learning tool designed to help Loco Pilots understand complex yard layouts, signals, points, and diversions through an interactive computer-based simulation.

THE CHALLENGE

Understanding a complex yard layout and remembering signal locations, points, and possible routes can be difficult when learning relies heavily on physical visits and memory.

THE SOLUTION

The Yard Simulator brings the yard layout onto a computer screen and allows users to simulate signals and explore railway routes, points, and diversions in a visual learning environment.

PRACTICAL VALUE

Makes yard familiarisation more visual and accessible, helping crew members strengthen their understanding before applying that knowledge in operational settings.

Visual Simulation Yard Layouts Signal Knowledge

The central idea was to make railway yard learning easier to visualise. Instead of depending entirely on memory or hand-drawn diagrams, users can view the yard on screen and explore signal indications and route arrangements through simulation.

05 Data Analytics
📊

SPM Data Analysis App

Turning Railway Speed Data into Meaningful Insights

A digital analysis platform designed to simplify the processing, examination, and reporting of Speedometer (SPM) data, helping transform raw operational records into structured information for review and analysis.

THE CHALLENGE

SPM files contain time-based operational data that can be difficult to review efficiently. Manual preparation, identifying relevant running sessions, examining speed behaviour, and preparing reports can require substantial effort.

THE SOLUTION

The application provides a structured workflow for processing uploaded data, preparing time-series information, analysing running sessions, examining speed-related events, and generating reports.

ANALYTICAL CAPABILITIES

Data preparation and session grouping, speed-profile analysis, overspeed examination against applicable benchmarks, identification of selected operational events, and structured Excel/PDF reporting.

PRACTICAL VALUE

Makes large volumes of SPM data easier to organise and examine, reduces repetitive analytical preparation, and supports a more consistent approach to operational review.

Python Streamlit Pandas Data Analysis Excel PDF Reporting
Watch SPM App Demonstration ↗
Explore the project in detail

The SPM Data Analysis App reflects the combination of railway domain knowledge and software development. Its purpose is not merely to display raw data, but to organise it into a form that is easier to analyse and interpret.

A typical analytical workflow involves preparing date-and-time information, grouping related records into running sessions, examining speed behaviour, identifying selected events, and preparing structured reports.

The application uses Python and Pandas for data processing, Streamlit for its web-based interface, and PDF-generation tools for reporting. Its design can be extended as analytical requirements evolve.

The broader objective is to make SPM data analysis more systematic, accessible, and useful for railway operational review.

08 Engineering Approvals
⚡

EIG Application

Digital Approval & Equipment Testing Management

A digital approval management application designed for the Traction Distribution Department to streamline PCEE approvals for new installations of OHE, SP, SSP, AT, and other traction power distribution infrastructure.

THE CHALLENGE

Obtaining approvals for new installations involves multiple testing formats, equipment readings, supporting documents, technical verification, and a defined approval hierarchy. Managing these activities manually can make tracking, coordination, and timely decision-making more difficult.

THE SOLUTION

The application digitises the approval workflow by allowing users to complete required forms, upload prescribed formats, submit testing and trial records, and route proposals through the established approval hierarchy for PCEE approval.

TECHNICAL VALIDATION & ALERTS

Equipment test readings are compared against configured threshold values. Alerts help administrators identify readings that require attention, supporting timely technical review before approval.

APPROVAL & WORK MONITORING

The application generates a consolidated one-page Approval Booklet and enables administrators to monitor approval status, track work completion, and identify the executing agency currently responsible for the work.

Digital Approvals OHE & Traction Assets Equipment Testing Threshold Alerts Approval Booklet Work Tracking
Explore the project in detail

The EIG Application provides a structured digital workflow for processing engineering approval proposals related to new installations of Overhead Equipment (OHE), Sectioning Posts (SP), Sub-Sectioning Posts (SSP), Auxiliary Transformers (AT), and other applicable traction distribution assets.

Users can complete the required forms, upload prescribed documents, and record equipment testing and trial readings. The application routes proposals through the defined approval hierarchy, helping ensure that the required stages of review and approval are followed.

A key feature is the automated comparison of equipment test readings against configured threshold values. When a reading falls outside the specified limits, the application can alert administrators so that the result can be reviewed and appropriate action taken.

The application also generates a consolidated one-page Approval Booklet, simplifying the presentation of essential approval information. Administrative monitoring provides visibility into the current status of proposals, work completion, and the executing agency responsible for the work.

By bringing documentation, technical checks, approval routing, and work-status monitoring into one application, the EIG Application makes the approval process more streamlined, transparent, and easier to track.
